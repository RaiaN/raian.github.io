FilmAgent

AI co-pilot for filmmakers. It covers screenwriting, asset generation, specialized AI skills and shot editing inside one project, driven by natural language and precise editing.

Left: the sessionThe director asks in plain words. Every step the agent takes shows as it happens, then a short report.
Right: the filmPreview, Generations and Library. They only view and organise: sort, filter, tag.
The director decidesThe agent reports what it made and what it saw. Picks, rejects and notes are the director's.

What it does

ScreenwritingAn idea becomes a screenplay, then a shot list: scenes, shots, and who and where each needs.
Asset generationCast, locations and props are drawn as plates and reused in every shot.
Video generationTakes of 5 to 30 s with sound. Drafts at 480p, then the pick is finished at 1080p.
Specialized skillsPreviz: a blocking schematic, then an animatic. Storyboard: the script on one sheet.
Editing shotsA note changes a finished take; framing and timing stay. Draw on a picture to say where.

How it works

  • Goal to steps. The agent plans each request and picks its tools. There is no fixed pipeline.
  • 30 tools. Screenplay, shot list, cast, variations, storyboard, previz, video, edit, finish, assemble.
  • Skills. Each model's prompt spec and each tool's prompts are plain files that can be customized.
  • Project context. One cloud film holds the script, shots, plates and takes. Studio and Canvas open the same film.
  • Checks its own work. It looks at frames of every result before reporting, and says what it could not check, such as sound.

Models and limits

VideoSeedance 2.5. Takes of 5 to 30 s with sound, 480p drafts, 1080p finals. 4K finals need Seedance 2.5 Premium.
ImagesSeedream 5.0 Pro by default, or Seedream 5.5. Up to 4K and up to 10 references.
PlannerSeed 2.1 Turbo by default; Seed 2.0 Pro or Seed-Evolving can be selected per film.
Per requestUp to 60 tool calls. A failed render is reported with its reason and is not retried silently.
DeploymentA Next.js app. Model endpoints on BytePlus ModelArk are set by environment variables; projects and media live in a TOS bucket.
